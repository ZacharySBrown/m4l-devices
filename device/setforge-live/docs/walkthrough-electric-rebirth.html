Wait about 5 seconds for pre-load to finish. The stems are streaming into Live's audio engine in the background. If you enter dual-song mode before pre-load completes, there may be a brief delay.

Tricks to try

Drum-swap call-and-response: Alternate tapping row 1 col 1 and row 1 col 2 every bar. Tribe's drums shift between MAIN and ALT-1 while Rebirth holds steady underneath.

Bass drop: Stop both basses (tap their active pads). Let just the dual drums ride for 4 bars. Then bring Rebirth's bass back on the downbeat (row 6, col 1). Instant drop.

Vox trade: Have Tribe vox going. Stop it (tap the active pad). Immediately tap Rebirth vox. The half-bar quant on vox means the handoff happens on the 2 or the 4.

Strip down: Stop everything except one drum and one bass from opposite songs. Minimal but groovy.

6. Exit and save

Tap the top-right side button to exit dual-song mode
Returns to your previous view (solo or per-row). Held chops from dual-song keep playing.
To stop everything: triple-tap PANIC (left side, bottom button)
All audio stops. All modes reset to solo. Clean slate.
To save your clip edits: send save to the device
Reads all clip markers from Live, writes manifest + set.json back to disk. Your edits round-trip.

7. Session workflow

The loop

3. Play, explore, find combinations that work
4. Tweak clip boundaries in Live's clip view if needed
5. Send save to persist changes back to the manifest
